Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse our products, select the item you want, choose the required options or quantity where applicable, and proceed to checkout.

Can I cancel my order after placing it?

Orders cannot be cancelled once confirmed. Please review your order carefully before making payment.

Do you offer refunds?

No. All purchases are final. A refund will only be issued if an item you have already paid for is sold out or unavailable.

What happens if my item is sold out?

If an item you have already paid for is unavailable, we will contact you and arrange a refund for that item.

Can I exchange an item?

No. We currently do not offer exchanges, so please ensure you select the correct item before completing your purchase.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times depend on your location and the delivery option selected at checkout. Estimated delivery time is 24–72 hours.

Can I change my delivery information after placing an order?

Contact us as soon as possible if you notice an error. Once an order has been dispatched, changes may no longer be possible.
